Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daisuke Nishikata (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game Virus Nurse Wars (ウイルス・ナースウォーズ) (the “App”).

1. Information we collect

(a) No account required. The App does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the App.

(b) Game data stored on your device. Your game progress, settings, and purchase status are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the App.

(c) Advertising (Google AdMob). The App shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • device model, operating system version, and language settings
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was displayed or tapped)
  • the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google’s own privacy policy. We do not receive information that directly identifies you.

(d) In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple ID. We receive only the information needed to confirm a purchase and deliver the item (such as the product ID and transaction status).

(e) Contacting us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the App, deliver purchased items, show ads (including personalized ads if you allow tracking), respond to inquiries, and prevent fraud and abuse.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy / How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Apple App Store (in-app purchases) — Apple Privacy Policy

If we add other advertising partners in the future, we will update this policy.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: You can allow or deny tracking when the App first asks. You can change this later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regions): Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Deleting data: You can delete all game data by deleting the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and for record-keeping. We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle.

7. International transfers

Information collected by Google may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information, the GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Please contact us using the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the “Last updated” date.
