FAQ

Will I lose my progress if I delete the App?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the App deletes it. We cannot restore it.

I bought an item but didn’t receive it.
Restart the App with an internet connection. For one-time purchases (such as ad removal), use Restore Purchases in the Settings screen. If it still doesn’t arrive, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

The App says it can’t connect to the network.
The App requires an internet connection. Please check your connection and tap Reconnect.
